Print quality

Why does resolution matter?

A standee is printed many times bigger than your phone screen, so every printed inch needs enough pixels from your photo. The score compares your photo's pixels with the size you've chosen: 90+ is great, 60+ good, 36+ fair, and below that it will likely look blurry or blocky.

Original photos from a phone camera (3000–4000 px) score well. Screenshots and images saved from chat apps or social media are usually under 1000 px — those can't score above "Fair", whatever the size.

Tip: upload the original photo (not a screenshot or a forwarded copy), or choose a smaller size.
