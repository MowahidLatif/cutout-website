Policies

Returns & refunds

All sales are final. Every standee is printed and cut to order from your photo, so we can't take it back, resell it, or offer refunds or exchanges.

Arrived damaged or misprinted? Email us a photo within 7 days of delivery and we'll reprint it free.

Why there are no returns

Your standee is made for you alone: your photo, your height, your text. Once it's printed it can't be used for anyone else. That's why every order is final sale, and you confirm you understand this before you pay.

This includes, for example:

  • changing your mind after ordering
  • choosing a different height, width, material or quantity
  • the photo you uploaded (its quality, cropping, expression or background)
  • typos in text you added

Damaged or misprinted standees

If your standee arrives damaged in shipping, or doesn't match what you ordered (for example, the wrong photo, wrong size or a printing defect), we'll make it right with a free reprint.

  • Email support@example.com within 7 days of delivery.
  • Include your order ID and a photo showing the damage or the problem.
  • Please keep the standee and packaging until we've replied.

Before you order

The studio shows your standee to scale before you buy. Please check the preview, the height, the width and any text carefully, and use the clearest, highest-resolution photo you have. What you see in the preview is what we print.

Changes and cancellations

If you spot a mistake right after ordering, email us straight away. Once production has started, orders can't be changed or cancelled.

Contact

Questions about an order? Email support@example.com with your order ID.
